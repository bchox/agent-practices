# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Treat people with respect; give constructive feedback; and assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior clearly.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be handled promptly and as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or access when needed to protect the community.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
